Equity Curve — Monte Carlo (600 Simulations)?
Median Top 5% Bottom 5%
Each line is a possible account journey. Because wins & losses arrive in random order, we run 600 sequences: the green median is your typical path, the blue/red bands show the luckiest and unluckiest 5%. Hover to read any trade point.
Outcome Distribution?
Where all 600 final balances landed. Bars right of the dashed start line finished in profit (green); bars left finished in loss (red). A tall right-skew means the odds favour you.
Underwater Curve — Median Drawdown %?
How far below your peak balance you typically sink. Deeper valleys = more painful losing streaks to sit through. This is the emotional cost of the strategy — can you stomach it?
Sensitivity — Profit vs Win Rate?
Your win rate
How total profit changes if only your win rate moved (R:R fixed). The yellow dot is you. Where the line crosses zero is your breakeven — see how much cushion you have above it.
Expectancy Heat — Win Rate × Risk:Reward?
Every combination of win rate (→) and reward ratio (↑). Green zones make money, red zones lose. It shows the trade-off: a low win rate can still win big if your reward per trade is large enough.
Capital Flow — Wins vs Losses vs Fees?
A waterfall of where money comes and goes: gross wins, minus losses, minus fees, equals your net. Handy for spotting when commissions quietly eat the edge.
